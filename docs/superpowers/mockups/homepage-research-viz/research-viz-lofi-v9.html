Continuous state, context-dependent signals

The inner state is one calm continuous grey line — always there, never directly visible. We approximate it with the bold blue line, built from many diverse signals. Crucially, no signal is best in general: at each junction the three families each contribute (fixed body / behavior / asking slots), but the heavyweight — the big dot that tightens the band most — rotates with the question. Behavior carries the most unique information for what they'll do next; the body carries it for a choice; what someone says carries it for how they'll feel. Every signal is still used, and the nudge acts on the state itself, toward creating, staying on track, and change.
