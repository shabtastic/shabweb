Homepage research visual — which direction?

Three lo-fi takes on how to picture your research program on the homepage. Click the one that resonates (or the closest), then tell me in the terminal what to push further — you can also mix elements across them.

what it communicates

Illustration of the phenomenon

A sketch of the science itself: noisy raw signals over nested timescales feeding into one inferred latent-state line (dashed where uncertain, solid where inferred). Research areas are pinned exactly where they act. Character: vivid, honest, immediately conveys the "hidden state over time" problem — leads with the phenomenon, not the org chart.

Diagram of the program logic

A concept map: three methods feed a central "latent states over time & in context" node, which flows out to three application areas, with the 8 named areas orbiting where they belong. Character: legible and complete — reads as a research statement at a glance, but more org-chart than phenomenon.

C · Hybrid, phenomenon-led

Hybrid, phenomenon-led

Option A's illustration as the base, but annotated with the program's structure — elicitation over the sources, triangulation where they converge, computational models at the inference step, and applications at the downstream end. Character: keeps the vivid phenomenon while making the method logic legible; the 8 area names move to a caption or hover instead of crowding the figure.
