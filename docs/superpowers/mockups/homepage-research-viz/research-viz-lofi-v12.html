how it composes

The prediction: state → outcome

The full chain now reads left to right: many diverse signals, related in a partly-known web, triangulate the hidden inner state into the bold estimate. At 'now' the estimate projects forward — a dotted forecast cone that widens with uncertainty — toward an outcome we actually care about: a choice, a habit that sticks, a creative leap. Two futures branch from the same state: without intervening, the ghost path drifts to a lesser outcome; the nudge, applied at the right moment, bends the trajectory to the better one. The true state itself fades at 'now' — the future is ours to predict, not to observe. Underneath, the timescales are drawn as nested brackets centred on 'now' rather than boxes over the past: most of the signal lives at seconds, hours and days, and each rate both looks back over its own window and buys a matching reach into the future — seconds for right now, years for the long horizon.
