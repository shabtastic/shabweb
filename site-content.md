## About — body (3 paragraphs)

> I'm a Senior Research Scientist in Human-Centered AI at the Toyota Research Institute. My research sits at the intersection of behavioral science and AI, combining theory- and data-driven approaches to investigate how context modulates learning and decision-making. I translate this work to building new technologies that help individuals and organizations meet their goals.

> I'm particularly interested in motivation, flexible self-regulation, and developing personalizable interventions for individual behavior change. I use diverse methods including neuroimaging, physiological monitoring, and experience sampling to sample and understand individual experience in context. I develop novel elicitation methods and computaional models, prioritizing diverse signals that evolve over different time scales to improve understanding of complex psychological phenomena and identify new, more precise targets for intervention.

> My current work focuses on two questions: 
1. Can novel preference elicitation methods be used to improve consumer preference prediction and forecasting, especially for innovative products?
2. Can neuroscience be used to guide generative AI and develop more personalizable, effective interventions to support psychological flexibility and creative decision making?

## Flexible Self-Regulation

[DESCRIPTION — edit below]
Self-regulation is a learnable skill, and the circuits that support it can be trained. This work uses real-time fMRI neurofeedback to teach people to volitionally regulate the ventral tegmental area — a core node in the brain's motivation system. An accompanying theoretical framework formalizes the conditions under which regulatory strategies generalize across goals and contexts.

Representative papers:
- Hakimi & Hare 2015 — Enhanced neural responses to imagined primary rewards predict reduced monetary temporal discounting (J Neuroscience)
- Hakimi, Hare & Rangel 2014 — Activity in dlPFC and its effective connectivity to vmPFC are associated with temporal discounting (Frontiers in Neuroscience)
- Hakimi et al. 2018 — Temporal structure of learning to regulate ventral tegmental area using real-time neurofeedback (CCN)
- Theory paper in preparation

## Agent State Inference
[NEW THEME — no draft yet, all copy below is a starting point, please rewrite in your voice.]
